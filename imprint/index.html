About YarnSpace

YarnSpace is a knitting and crochet app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, developed and published by Julian Lau as JL Apps. It brings row counters, your yarn stash, Ravelry patterns, yarn substitutes and calculators together in one app, works without an account and doesn't track you. Questions, feedback and bug reports are welcome by email.

Privacy

YarnSpace and this website collect no personal data for advertising or analytics and set no tracking cookies.
