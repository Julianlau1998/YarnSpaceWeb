Where to start

Most questions in a knitting or crochet project come back to two numbers: your gauge and the length of yarn you have. That's why the gauge swatch guide is a good first read. It shows how to knit or crochet a swatch, wash and block it like the finished piece, and count stitches and rows over 10 cm or 4 inches without fooling yourself.

Once you know your gauge, the other guides build on it. Want to use a different yarn than the pattern calls for? Substituting yarn compares weight, fiber and meters per gram, and works out how many skeins to buy. Not sure the stash is enough? How much yarn you need calculates it from the pattern, or from a swatch when you're designing your own piece.

When a pattern says "increase 14 stitches evenly across the row", increasing or decreasing evenly tells you exactly where each one goes. The needle and hook size chart converts metric, US and UK sizes for patterns from anywhere. And photo to chart turns a picture into a chart for colorwork, tapestry crochet or cross stitch.

The calculators in YarnSpace

Every guide explains the math so you can do it with pen and paper. If you'd rather not, YarnSpace for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch has calculators for gauge, cast-on, yarn amounts and increasing or decreasing evenly, right next to your projects, your row counters and your yarn stash. You don't need an account to use them. New guides are added regularly, always with worked examples and practical tips.
